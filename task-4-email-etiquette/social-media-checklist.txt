TASK-4B:
SOCIAL MEDIA CHECKLIST:
Do’s
*  Think before posting — ensure content is respectful and appropriate 
*  Keep your account privacy settings updated 
*  Use social media to build a positive digital presence (projects, achievements) 
*  Verify information before sharing to avoid spreading misinformation 
*  Be respectful in comments, messages, and discussions 
Don’ts
*  Don’t share sensitive personal information (address, phone number, passwords) 
*  Don’t post offensive, abusive, or inappropriate content 
*  Don’t engage in cyberbullying or online arguments 
*  Don’t accept friend/follow requests from unknown or suspicious accounts 
*  Don’t overshare your daily location or activities publicly
