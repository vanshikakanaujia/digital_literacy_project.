Stay Safe Online – Checklist for College Students (India)
?? Use strong, unique passwords for every account (mix of letters, numbers, symbols)
?? Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on Gmail, Instagram, banking apps, etc.
?? Never click on unknown links from emails, WhatsApp, or SMS (even if they look official)
?? Avoid using public Wi-Fi for banking or payments
?? Keep your apps and phone updated to avoid security vulnerabilities
?? Do not share OTP, PIN, or passwords with anyone — not even “bank officials”
UPI & Financial Safety Tips
?? Never approve unknown UPI collect/payment requests (they can steal money)
?? Remember: receiving money does NOT require entering a PIN
?? Verify QR codes before scanning — fake QR codes can redirect payments
?? Use only official apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) from trusted app stores
 Smart Online Behavior
?? Keep social media profiles private and avoid oversharing personal details
?? Be cautious while downloading files — they may contain malware
Report Cybercrime in India
If you become a victim or notice suspicious activity:
National Cyber Crime Portal: https://cybercrime.gov.in
Helpline Number: 1930
